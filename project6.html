Hobby 3D Printing

I have been 3D printing as a hobby for the past year, and have learned a lot about the limitations of 3D printing and how to design for manufacturability and account for tolerances. I have designed and printed a variety of parts, from functional parts for my own projects to decorative items and presents for friends and family.

Stool Project

Built a stool for my room from scratch. Designed the stool in Fusion 360, and 3D printed a jig to hold the complex geometry in place to allow for cutting and welding at the precise angles needed. MIG welded all the parts together and taught my friends to weld as well! Made the stool top out of scrap wood pieces.

Model Rocketry

Built and launched a model rocket with an E class engine, helping me understand the basics of rocket design

Key Engineering Contributions

  • CAD Design: Used Solidworks and Fusion 360 to create CAD models for 3D printed parts, and taking into account the limitations and capabilities of the 3D printing process. Printed several iterations of the design to test strength and tolerances of designed parts
  • Design for manufacturing: Develeoped and understanding of how to make CAD models come to life. Understanding that what can be built in CAD is not always what can be built in reality.
  • Manufacturing: Gained hands-on experience with various manufacturing processes, including 3D printing, welding, and machining. Understood the importance of considering manufacturing constraints during the design phase.
  • Rocketry: Helped me understand the basics of rocketry, allowing me to be successful in RPL and Daedalus, and fueling my interest in aerospace.

Results & Impact

Enhanced my understanding of 3D printing technologies and design principles, and has helped me bring my creative ideas to life through tangible prototypes, allowing me to dip my toes into the engineering world more as I used my skills to build my custom FPV drone. I also have greatly enjoyed being able to 3D print personalized presents adn gifts for my friends, and it is wonderful to see my friends and family enjoy the things I have made for them through 3D printing. Additionally, building the stool from scratch was the first project I had undertaken and I am so proud of how much I learned through it and the final project. It has lived in my dorm room and apartment througout college, and hopefully will continue to do so for the rest of my life.
