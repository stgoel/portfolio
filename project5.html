SLAC Internship

Jul – Sep 2025, Jun - Aug 2026

Electronics Testing Structural Analysis Software Data Analysis

Project Overview & Problem Statement

During my internship at SLAC, I was involved in the testing, diagnosis, analysis and repair of over 50 Beam Positioning monitors boards. These boards are critical for determining the position of the beam inside the accelerator, allowing for better images. I was also involved in 3D printing and modeling PCB covers and strain relief hardware and printing out of PEEK, a radioactively hard material. I also designed a workbench for a 200lb laser stripper made out of over the counter 80-20 parts and performed FEA to ensure structural integrity. Built and commisioned the table on sight. Additionally, tested signal integrity of high data transmission cables that are over 6m long used for the ATLAS project at CERN and verified impedances and S-parameters.

Key Engineering Contributions

  • Electronics: Used Time Domain Reflectometer and Newtork Analyzer and Active probe to diagnose and repair over 50 BPM boards.
  • FEA: Conducted finite element analysis in Fusion 360 to ensure that the table could supprt the intended load of the laserstripper
  • 3D printing: Used INTAMSUITE PEEK printer and software to print PCB covers and strain relief hardware. 3D printing the strain relief hardware helped reduce the cost, as these were originally CNCed from steel and were expensive to manufacture.
  • Software: Created a program in python to collect and analyze real time beam data and calculate resolutions of BPMs, allowing for in-situ diagnosis of BPM issues. Built program from scratch and evaluated 4 methods of resolution calculations and proposed one of them as the most effective diagnostic tool. Published in paper for International Beam Instrumentation Conference, where I will be attending and presenting my work in a poster session in September 2026.

Results & Impact

Fixed over 50 BPM boards and improved their performance, contributing to the overall reliability of the accelerator systems. The 3D printed components reduced manufacturing costs and lead times. The FEA analysis ensured the structural integrity of the workbench, allowing for safe operation under the intended load conditions, and which is now being used by technicians! Published my first paper and attended my first conference!
