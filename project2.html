MAE3

March 2025 – Present

CAD / Solidworks Design for Manufacturing Medical Robotics

Project Overview & Problem Statement

As an undergraduate researcher, I worked on designing and manufacturing a 3D Resin Printed notched wrist tendon driven mechanism with a soft everting vine robot for endovascular navigation through a Type 2 Aortic Arch. The main challenge was designing a mechanism that could fit within the small diameter of the surgical introducer (4mm). I also worked on implementing and optimizing the best lasercutter settings for laserwelding TPU and characterize its performance via tensile T-peel tests.

Key Engineering Contributions

  • Design: Took on the most challengin mechanism and designed a 4 staged cascading linear slider mechanism to allow us to score on the highest rung of Sun God.
  • Analysis: Performed torque energy and power analysis to ensure that the motors could raise the linear slider mechanism to the required height. Discovered that while the motors were sufficient to move the sliders, the movement would be very slow, so rather than having two sliders on each side of the robot, having one and using two motors to drive it. This made it so the time it took to extend our slides fully was less than 5 seconds, making it one of the fastest mechanisms in the class.
  • Design for fabrication: Iteratively protoyped until a functional design was achieved. Many early designs failed due to the lack of consideration of manufacturing and assembly, and inability to produce low tolerance parts as everything was lasercut and fabricated in house
  • Competition: My team was the only team to successfully implement a linear slider mechanism that could reach the top rung of Sun God. We placed in the Top 12 of teams.

Results & Impact

By routing external steering tendons and integrating radio-opaque ink markers, steering performance and real-time fluoroscopic visibility during surgical procedures were significantly improved. Additionally, I constructed a parametric spreadsheet model to optimize dimensions for antagonistic steering in subsequent iterations.
