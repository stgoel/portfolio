RPL

October 2024 – Present

Structural Analysis Design for Manufacturing Welding

Project Overview

Joined the RPL new member team, Daedalus, and learned the basics of rocketry. Designed and launched a rocket intended to reach an altitude of 3500+ft and Mach 0.6 using Solidworks, FEA fluid analysis, OpenRocket, a G-class motor, 3D-printing, and an Arduino Nano.

Enjoyed my experienced in Daedalus so I joined the structures team for RPL's liquid bipropellant rocket, Phoenix. As a Structures RE I am responsible for the external structure for launch hardware and analysis for next rocket iteration.

Key Engineering Contributions

  • Design: Designed external structure, taking into considerations for avionics and RF interference. Designed 20 inch aluminum 180 degree sections for the body tube that will be bolted to the internal structure, based on manufacturing capabilities. Designed a fiberglass tube and internal structure attatchement and tested RF interference for avionics bay section of the rocket.
  • Manufacturing: Manufactured boat tail and body tube panels in house using stock 0.63" aluminum sheets. Lasercut and waterjet the panels and sheet metal rolled. Sheet metal rolling the boat tail which is tapered was not easy, but came up with a manufacturing procedure to allow for tapered sections to be manufaactured
  • Analysis: Performed structural analysis on current tanks to evaluate potential load bearing performance for next iteration of rocket. Load bearing tanks would drastically reduce rocket weight as less internal structure is needed to support rocket components, allowing for higher and faster flight of rocket

Results & Impact

Through my experience in Daedalus and the Phoneix structures team, I gained valuable insights into rocket design and analysis, which has influenced me into pursuing a potential career in the aerospace industry.
