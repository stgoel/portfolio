Custom FPV Drone

June 2026 – June 2025

CAD Blade design Electronics

Project Overview

Designed and built a custom FPV drone. 3D printed drone frame with custom propeller designs and integrated electronic components. Used Teensy 4.0 microcontroller and MPU 6050 for flight control and navigation.

Key Engineering Contributions

  • Design: Developed the overall architecture and layout of the drone body, ensuring a light weight but strong structure. Created a spreadsheet calculator using Blade Energy Momemtum Theory to design custom propellers using NACA airfoil profiles.
  • Electronics: Designed and implemented the electronic systems for flight control and navigation, including the integration of the Teensy 4.0 microcontroller and MPU 6050 sensor.
  • Thrust Verifications: Conducted thrust testing and verification to ensure the custom propellers had enough thrust output to allow for flight.

Results & Impact

Learned the fundamentals of drone design and construction, including aerodynamics, electronics integration, and flight control systems. Understood the importance of electronic integration with structural design, and challenged myself to learn about microelectronics and drone design from scratch
